# For Gunjan, with love

An interactive birthday surprise for **12 January 2027**, following the supplied video. Open `index.html` in a browser to play. It works immediately, including before her birthday. No build or account is required.

Pull and release the bow (or tap the heart), open the present, explore the album, and knock three times to enter the museum. Inside are a scratch-off gallery, a nine-piece photo puzzle, a sealed birthday letter with a voice note, and five promises to sign. The music button plays “Lovers” by Anna of the North. The song pauses during the birthday voice note and resumes when it finishes.

## Add your photos

Replace `assets/photos/01.jpg` through `assets/photos/08.jpg` with your own JPEG photos, keeping the filenames. Square crops work well. The album and scratch gallery use all eight images. The puzzle uses `08.jpg`. Update the eight captions at the top of `birthday.js` if desired.

The prototype uses original illustrated artwork, sample still-life photographs, and a synthesized birthday note. To use your own voice, replace `assets/birthday-note.mp3`. The letter is in `index.html`.
